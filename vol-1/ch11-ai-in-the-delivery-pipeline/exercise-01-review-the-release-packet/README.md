# Exercise 1 — Review the release packet

Open the [proposed packet](starter/release-packet.md). Mark the evidence that
belongs to a different revision. Assign someone to review the exact build
and someone to make the decision about exposure. Specify what each probe
must check and what the operator will observe after rollback.

**Verify** whether someone on call could tell when to proceed, hold, stop,
and declare recovery from your packet. Compare your notes with the
[review notes](solution/review.md) and the [completed packet](../docs/release-packet.md).
